# Discussion-about-AI-CMT-UZH

A shared repository for thoughts, questions, opinions, and proposals concerning AI, its use. It should provide a systematic basis for summarizing previous Journal Club discussions, planning future meetings, and preparing concrete points to raise with external entities about the short-, medium-, and long-term implications of AI.

Feel free to contribute at your own time.

## Repository structure

| Directory | Purpose |
| --- | --- |
| `proposals/short-term/` | Practical concerns, institutional limitations, open questions, and suggestions for near-term changes. |
| `overviews/` | Summaries of discussions following each Journal Club meeting. |
| `questions/` | Questions and discussion points the group would like to address to external entities. |
| `opinions/` | Open-ended reflections, personal perspectives, and idealistic visions concerning AI. |
| `opinions/short-term/` | Perspectives relevant on a time-scale of our current positions and the period of next-likely employment. |
| `opinions/medium-term/` | Perspectives on subsequent career stages and changes to research, education, and academic institutions. |
| `opinions/long-term/` | Perspectives on broader changes to society, science, employment. |

The structure will be iteratively revised to make sharing and systemizing ideas and organizing discussions more effective. Suggestions for improvement are welcome.

## Proposals

The `proposals/short-term/` directory is devoted to practical concerns about the effects of AI on our work, professional roles, and institutional structures. Due to high unpredictability `proposals/` section should contain only short-term ideas. Broader medium- and long-term reflections belong in the corresponding opinion directories. Please restrain more idealistic views for the `opinions/`section.

Contributions should identify relevant problems, worries, or open questions, such as:

- Where current institutional structures may no longer be adequate, and what would need to change for them to remain viable.
- How researchers can maintain the relevance of their work and redefine their professional roles.
- How the role, objectives, and expectations of a PhD may need to change (both for PhD students and supervisors).
- What guidelines or limits should apply to AI use in research and teaching.
- How teaching and training should adapt.

Personal suggestions for resolving these problems are welcome but optional. Clearly identifying a concern or an unresolved question is itself a useful contribution.

### How to contribute

Create a subfolder with your name under `proposals/short-term/` as `proposals/short-term/your-name/`, containing:

- **`main.md`** — State your assessment of the current situation, your relevant concerns, and the problems or open questions you would like the group to address. Include proposed solutions if you have them. Begin with a short bullet list of your main points, then develop your reasoning in the sections below.
- **`query.md`** — Other contributors can suggest responses to your open questions, comment on your proposed solutions, and raise additional questions. Use the template below.

For example, Alice creates the folder `proposals/short-term/Alice/` with files `main.md` which is modified solely by Alice and an empty `query.md` such that it is mainly written with contributions of Not-Alice (Alice may only respond to the questions). 

## Overviews

After each Journal Club discussion, a dated summary of the discussion will be added to `overviews/`, for example `discussion-dd-mm-yyyy.md`.

Each overview will capture the main topics discussed, points of agreement and disagreement, resolved and unresolved questions, proposed follow-up. It should describe the current state of the discussion without implying consensus where none exists, and help identify topics for future meetings.

## Questions for external discussions

The `questions/` directory collects questions and discussion points that the group would like to raise with people or organizations outside the group, such as representatives of AI companies, social scientists, policymakers, and/or people working on educational and institutional reform.

These questions will be organized in a single collective dated file `question-dd-mm-yyyy.md` by topic or intended audience. Questions about an individual contribution, including those intended for a future Journal Club meeting, belong in the relevant contributor's `query.md` file.

## Opinions

The `opinions/` section welcomes personal reflections, concerns, arguments, and visions about AI, including speculative or idealistic perspectives. Contributions are free to explore all idealistic, utopian/dystopian ideas, therefore, state whatever contributors expect, hope for, or consider desirable across different time horizons.

### How to contribute

Choose the relevant time horizon and create a subfolder with your name as `opinions/short-term/your-name/`, containing:

- **`main.md`** — Present your statement or position. Begin with a short bullet list of your main points, then develop your reasoning in the sections below. Include examples or references where useful.
- **`query.md`** — Other contributors can collect questions, requests for clarification, alternative perspectives, and counterarguments (antitheses) to your statement. These entries will serve as discussion points for the next suitable Journal Club meeting.


## Query file template

Use the following structure for `query.md` in both the proposals and opinions sections. Each person responding should add a separate section under their own name.

```markdown
# Query

## Your name

### Suggestions

- Add your suggestions, possible solutions, alternative perspectives, or counterarguments here 
related to the statement provided by author of the main.md file.

### Questions

- Add the questions related to the main.md statement you would like the author or the group 
to address here.
```

Include your name in `main.md`, and date your contributions to `query.md`. Keep each person's contributions clearly attributed.
